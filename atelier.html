TOPIC 01 Optics Instrumentation · Free-Space Alignment

The Experimental Art of Beam Walking: Decoupling Spatial and Angular Degrees of Freedom

A freely propagating laser beam possesses four spatial degrees of freedom that cannot be independently steered by a single mirror without severe clipping. We detail the two-mirror feedback algorithm through dual pinhole apertures, cross-talk compensation, and thermal drift recovery for 4 Kelvin confocal cryostats.

TOPIC 02 2D Materials · Moiré Superlattices

Strain Recovery & Exciton Dehybridization in Twisted TMDC Bilayers

Interfacial hydrocarbon bubbles and inhomogeneous strain quench moiré optical quality in van der Waals heterostructures. We examine contact-mode AFM mechanical ironing to sweep defects, recover sub-nanometer atomic contact, and sharpen momentum-indirect exciton linewidths in MoSe₂/MoS₂.

TOPIC 03 Quantum Sensing · Solid-State Spins

Coherent Spin Readout: Optically Detected Magnetic Resonance in Diamond

Negatively charged nitrogen-vacancy (NV⁻) centers in nanodiamond provide atomic-scale quantum magnetometers. We demonstrate continuous-wave optical polarization into the ms=0 spin state, microwave ESR sweep at 2.87 GHz, and optical detection of Zeeman sublevel splitting.

TOPIC 04 Nanofabrication · Directed Assembly

Nanoscale Electrostatic Trapping: AFM Nanoxerography for Quantum Emitters

Deterministic coupling of single quantum emitters to dielectric metasurfaces requires sub-50 nm spatial accuracy. We demonstrate conductive AFM tip charge injection on PMMA thin films to create localized electrostatic charge templates that trap colloidal nanodiamonds.

TOPIC 05 Quantum Optics · Photon Statistics

HBT Interferometry: Verifying Single-Photon Antibunching

To prove that an emission source is a true quantum emitter rather than an ensemble, we measure the second-order photon autocorrelation function g⁽²⁾(τ) using a Hanbury Brown–Twiss interferometer. We analyze timing jitter, photon antibunching, and the hallmark g⁽²⁾(0) < 0.5 signature.
