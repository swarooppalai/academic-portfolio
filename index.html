01 / ABOUT

Research Profile & Biography

I am an experimental physicist specializing in quantum optics and nanophotonics, currently working at the intersection of quantum emitters and nanoscale photonic architectures.

My research focuses on controlling and enhancing the emission properties of nitrogen-vacancy (NV) centers in nanodiamonds by coupling them with dielectric and plasmonic metasurfaces. I bring deep expertise in AFM nanoxerography for deterministic nanoscale assembly, cryogenic spectroscopy, and custom optical instrumentation.

Prior to my postdoctoral work, my PhD at CNRS-LNCMI pioneered AFM ironing as a technique to improve the intrinsic optical quality of 2D semiconductor heterostructures, contributing to publications in Nano Letters and 2D Materials.

Research Experience & Projects

Post-Doctoral Projects

01

Plasmonic Optical Memristive Memory of an Addressable Processing Device (POMMARD)

CEMES & LPCNO, Toulouse, France 2026 — Present

  • Designing nanofabrication workflows and developing deterministic sample preparation protocols for directed assembly of NV centers.
  • Optimizing AFM nanoxerography surface charge patterning and positioning yields for single nanodiamonds near plasmonic Double Hexagon (DH) cavities.
  • Establishing experimental hybrid integration strategies of local quantum photon sources with on-chip plasmonic memristive architectures for interconnect-free optical logic units.
02

Quantum-Emitters-Embedded Dielectric Light-Emitting Metasurfaces (Q-META)

CEMES & LPCNO, Toulouse, France 2025 — 2026

  • Controlling and enhancing emission properties of quantum emitters (NV centers in nanodiamonds) using 2D optical dielectric metasurfaces hosting Bound In the Continuum States.
  • Deterministic positioning of nanodiamonds in the near field of dielectric arrays using directed assembly via AFM nanoxerography.
  • Optical characterization at the single photon scale including time-resolved confocal microscopy and photon correlation.
